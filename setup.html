Step 1

What is Soulseek?

Soulseek is a peer-to-peer (P2P) file-sharing network, popular for music. When you download a file, you're downloading it directly from another user's computer — not from a central server. Likewise, other users can download files from you.

Because it's P2P:
  • You need a Soulseek account to use it.
  • Downloads go faster when more users share the same file.
  • Users who share files get faster download speeds themselves.
  • In some countries (e.g. Russia), the network may need a VPN to reach reliably.
